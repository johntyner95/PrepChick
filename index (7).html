Upload item sales

Open item sales report

Upload a .csv or .html report that includes your soup, salad and wrap sales. It needs a date column, an item name column and a count sold column. An hour or time column makes the time-of-day estimate more accurate. Uploading again adds to what is saved and replaces matching dates. To add one day, set days to 1 and pick the date.

Upload hourly sales

Open hourly sales report

Upload your 15-minute or hourly sales report. It needs a date (or day) column, an hour or time column and a sales column. It shows the app how sales build through the day. Upload today’s partway through the day and the app adjusts for how busy you are. Uploading again adds to what is saved and replaces matching dates.

Trays on hand

Count what you have and add what you cook. The app tracks how old each tray is for the 48-hour limit. Counting lower removes the oldest trays first. Trays you add by counting higher are assumed to be 24 hours old.

History

Typical trays used per day, learned from your uploads. Each upload with dates adds to this, so Monday and Tuesday stay separate. To add a single day, set days to 1 and pick the date on the Upload Data tab. It needs at least 2 of the same weekday before it can tell weekdays apart. Until then it uses the recent average.

Forecast, next 7 days

Trays per day. It adjusts for the season using a year of your sales, so a monthly item upload is enough.

Settings

Open and close hours are used when the file has no hourly detail.

Chicken per sale (ounces)

Ounces of shredded chicken in one sale. The starting numbers come from your recipes. Change them if a recipe changes.

Manager code

The code keeps these pages from being changed by accident. It is saved on this device only, so set it on each phone or tablet. It is not strong security. Until you change it, the code is 1234.
